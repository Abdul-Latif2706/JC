ABOUT US

Crafting Confidence Since 2019

Founded on 24 November 2019, Jonnie’s Cutz was built with a clear vision — to redefine modern grooming and beauty through excellence and structure.

Based in the heart of Accra, Ghana, we are more than a barbershop. We are a refined grooming and beauty destination where craftsmanship meets experience.

We believe grooming is not routine — it is identity.
